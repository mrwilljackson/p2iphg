# Registration Flow

**Last Updated:** 2026-04-16

---

## For Attendees — What You'll See

When you visit the registration page, you first choose your role:

- **"I am a Participant"** — Individual attendees
- **"I am a Volunteer"** — Event helpers and staff
- **"I am a Group Leader"** — Teachers, parents, or community group leaders registering with a group

---

### Participant Registration (2 steps)

**Step 1:** Enter your first name, surname, and email address. Select your organisation from the dropdown. Answer whether you have an impairment.

**Step 2:** Consent checkboxes — photo consent (if you agree, you'll receive an orange wristband), feedback consent, and next event consent. Then submit.

---

### Volunteer Registration (1 step)

Enter your email address. The system looks you up automatically and finds your name and details (imported from Airtable before the event). Tick the consent checkboxes and submit. This is the quickest registration path.

---

### Group Leader Registration (3 steps)

**Step 1:** Enter your first name, surname, and email address.

**Step 2:** Select your organisation. If you're a known contact for that organisation, the system pre-fills your details. Enter your group size (the number of participants in your group, not including yourself), the number of disabled students, the number of SEN students, and whether you are personally participating in the event.

**Step 3:** Consent checkboxes — photo, feedback, and next event consent. Then submit.

---

## For Event Admins — What You Can See

Event administrators log in with the event admin PIN and can:

- **View all registrations** in real-time as they come in during the event
- **See participant counts** broken down by organisation, showing expected vs actual numbers
- **View group leader details** and their group sizes
- **Manually register** organisations or volunteers if needed on the day

---

## Smart Features

The system includes several automatic features to keep registration running smoothly:

**No duplicate group leaders** — Once an organisation's group leader has registered, that organisation is hidden from the group leader dropdown. This prevents two people accidentally registering as leader for the same group.

**No duplicate volunteers** — Volunteers who have already registered are automatically hidden from the volunteer name picker, so they can't register twice.

**Automatic group leader detection** — If someone registers as an individual participant but their email matches a known group leader, the system records this connection automatically. This ensures the group's participant numbers are tracked correctly even if the leader uses the wrong registration path.
